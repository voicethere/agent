# Products overview

## Tents and shelters

The Ridgeline 2 is a two-person three-season backpacking tent. The Basecamp 4 is a four-person tent for car camping with a tall ceiling and two doors.

## Sleep and packs

The Summit 20 is a 20 degree down sleeping bag. The Trailhead 45 is a 45 liter backpack with an adjustable torso.

## Footwear

The Talus Mid is a waterproof mid-height hiking boot.

## Cooking and water

The Ember Stove is a compact canister stove. The Clearflow Filter is a hollow-fiber water filter.

## Lighting

The Beacon Headlamp is a rechargeable headlamp with a red night mode.
