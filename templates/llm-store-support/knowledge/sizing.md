# Sizing guide

## Footwear

Boots and trail shoes run true to US sizing. If you are between sizes, order the larger one and wear the socks you hike in. A thumb's width of space in front of your longest toe is right for hiking boots. Wide fits are marked with a W.

## Jackets and base layers

Our jackets are cut with room for a fleece underneath. If you plan to wear only a base layer under the jacket, take your usual size. For a layering setup with a thick mid layer, size up. Base layers fit close to the body, so choose your normal size for a snug fit.

## Backpacks

Pack sizes are measured by torso length, not by height. Measure from the bump at the base of your neck to the top of your hip bones. A torso of 16 to 18 inches fits a small, 18 to 20 inches a medium and over 20 inches a large.

## Sleeping bags and tents

Regular sleeping bags fit people up to six feet tall. Long sizes fit up to six feet six inches. A tent's person count is a tight fit, so most people pick one size larger than the number of sleepers.
