# Warranty

## What the warranty covers

Kestrel Outdoor own-brand gear carries a limited lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. This covers broken zippers, failed seams, delaminated soles and cracked poles. Gear from other brands carries that brand's warranty, and we help you file the claim.

## What it does not cover

Normal wear, UV fading, damage from fire or misuse, and modifications are not covered. Waterproof coatings wear off with use; the care card in the package explains how to re-treat them.

## Making a claim

Contact support with the order number or a photo of the item and a description of the problem. We repair the item, replace it, or issue store credit, whichever fits the damage. Repairs take about two weeks plus shipping time.
