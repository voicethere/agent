# Acme Cloud pricing

Acme Cloud has three plans. The Free plan costs 0 dollars per month and includes one project and 1 GB of storage. The Starter plan costs 19 dollars per month and includes five projects and 50 GB of storage. The Team plan costs 99 dollars per month and includes unlimited projects, 500 GB of storage and priority support.

## Overage

Storage above the plan limit is billed at 0.10 dollars per GB per month. Bandwidth is free up to 1 TB per month, then 0.05 dollars per GB.
