# Shipping at Kestrel Outdoor

## Delivery times and carriers

Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Standard shipping takes three to five business days within the continental United States. Express shipping takes one to two business days. Orders to Alaska, Hawaii and US territories take up to ten business days.

## Shipping costs

Larger orders ship standard for free. The free-shipping threshold and the flat rates for smaller orders and for express are shown at checkout.

## Tracking and delivery problems

Every order gets a tracking link by email when it ships. If a package shows as delivered but did not arrive, wait one business day and check with neighbors, then contact support. We open a carrier claim and send a replacement once the claim is filed.

## International orders

We ship to Canada. Duties and import taxes are charged by the carrier on delivery. We do not ship to other countries yet.
